What We Do

Technical Services

GEC Consulting provides independent technical expertise across GIS, CAD, and environmental disciplines — delivering precise, analysis-driven deliverables for land surveyers, environmental firms, and private clients.

GIS & Spatial Analysis

Comprehensive geographic information system services including spatial data management, custom map production, geodatabase development, and site suitability modeling. Proficient in ArcGIS Pro, Drone2Map, and Python-based geoprocessing workflows.

ArcGIS Pro Drone2Map Spatial Modeling Remote Sensing Geodatabase

CAD Drafting & Design

Professional 2D and 3D CAD production for civil engineering, site planning, and infrastructure projects. Deliverables include site plans, grading plans, utility layouts, and as-builts — formatted to project and agency standards.

AutoCAD Civil 3D Site Plans Grading As-Builts

Environmental Consulting

Environmental field assessments, permit support, and regulatory compliance consulting for land development and infrastructure projects. Services include wetland delineation support, city and community code compliance, and buildable area calculation.

Wetlands Code Compliance Buildable Area Field Assessment Permitting

How It Works

The Engagement Process

01 // INTAKE

Project Scoping

We discuss your project requirements, timeline, deliverables, and budget to determine a clear scope of work and define measurable outcomes.

02 // PROPOSAL

Proposal & Agreement

A written proposal with itemized fees, a defined schedule, and a services agreement is issued for your review and approval before work begins.

03 // EXECUTION

Technical Work

Field work, data analysis, drafting, or report production proceeds according to the agreed scope with regular progress updates provided throughout.

04 // DELIVERY

Deliverables & Review

Final deliverables are submitted in agreed formats. A review period with revision allowances ensures every product meets your project requirements.
